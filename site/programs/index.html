Four programs · Three levels each

Every program rests on the same base: your room, breakfast every day, morning yoga in the shala above the valley, and afternoon tea with Balinese sweets. The levels (Enough / Present, Deeper Presence, Fully Present) add from there.

5 nights · For two, at the beginning

Hidden Honeymoon

Romantic room setting and the two-hour “Memory of Couple” spa ritual. Deeper adds the melukat purification at the sacred springs and one slow dinner. Fully Present adds a silver jewellery workshop, a sunrise trip to Kintamani with breakfast overlooking the volcano, and a photoshoot at the resort.

The price

Prices are for two people and for the whole stay, not per night, and they are what you pay booking with us directly. The figure shown is the lowest: the Enough / Present level in low season. A higher level, a busier month or a different room category moves it, and we tell you the exact number before you decide. Taxes and service are already inside the figure, so it is the whole sum.

For retreat organisers · Groups of 3–20

Host a retreat

The honest numbers first, because they are what you actually need:

  • Villa Oia is the core: sleeps 11 on its own grounds, one level, no steps; hotel rooms take the rest of the group.
  • The yoga shala takes up to 20: that is the ceiling of a group that still feels like a retreat.
  • The shala is the lowest point of the property, 26 steps down from the entrance. We say it now so you can plan for it.
  • Spaces are booked by agreed time slots; a few hours a day are included, exclusive half-days or full days cost extra.
  • Payment: 30% to confirm → 40% thirty days before arrival → 30% on the day. The price locks with the first payment.
  • Cancellation: half of whatever has been paid is retained, half comes back.
  • All meals come from our restaurant. Outside catering is not possible. Menus adapt: vegetarian, vegan, budget.
