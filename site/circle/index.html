The Hidden Circle

The members’ rate, on our own booking page only

What it is

The Hidden Circle is our list of guests and friends of the hotel. Members get a code. With the code, our booking page shows the members’ rate.

This rate is not published. Booking.com and other travel sites cannot show it. You see it only here, after you join.

How it works

  • Leave your email below. It takes ten seconds and costs nothing.
  • You see your code at once. We also send it to your email.
  • Press the button. Our booking page opens with the code already applied.
  • Choose your dates and your room. You see the exact price before you book.

What the price depends on

The members’ rate changes with the season, the room and the number of nights. The booking page always shows the real price for your dates, so there is nothing to calculate.
