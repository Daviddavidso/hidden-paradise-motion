The property

Six places to spend a day

Everything you eat is served on the west rim of the valley, which means every dinner comes with a sunset. Everything that slows you down (the spa, the shala) sits a flight of steps below, in the green.

Sunset Point Restaurant

Daily 07:30 – 22:30 · Breakfast, lunch & dinner

European cooking with a short, honest list of Indonesian classics, and the terrace faces west.

Hidden Gem Lounge & Bar

Daily 15:00 – 23:00 · Cocktails & an art exhibition

On the Gem floor, level with the spa and the Gem 2-Bedroom Villa, with no steps between them.

In your suite

Floating breakfast

The most photographed breakfast on the property, in your own pool or in the main one.

Hidden Gem Spa

Daily 09:00 – 21:00

The riverside shala

An open pavilion above the gorge. No walls, just the canopy and the sound of the river.

In your suite

When you would rather not move

Room service runs from 07:30 to 22:00 at no delivery charge. Call reception or send a WhatsApp message and the kitchen brings it over. Breakfast is served at the restaurant from 07:30 to 11:00, and it can just as easily arrive on your own terrace.

Floating breakfast · In the water

Breakfast that floats

A decorated tray arrives in the water in front of you. It is the most photographed breakfast on the property. If your suite has a pool of its own, it comes there; if not, the main infinity pool hosts it just as gladly.

Served from 8:00 to 11:30. The setting and the tray are IDR 100,000 for one and 200,000 for two; the breakfast itself is ordered from the menu as usual. No need to book ahead. The tray is set while the kitchen makes the breakfast. Ask at reception or by WhatsApp.

The pool
Open 07:00 – 21:00, towels at the poolside, and the far edge disappears into the jungle.
