The resort

Two hectares of valley,
and no one else in it

Twenty suites and villas stand along the west rim of the Petanu gorge, in a working Balinese village, a ten-minute drive from central Ubud. Every room faces the sunset. Below the terrace the river runs through forest that has never been cleared, the same forest the long-tailed macaques cross at dusk on their way upstream.

The resident macaques

They live here · Not a tour

The troop lives along the gorge and wanders the grounds on its own schedule: gentle with people, quick with unattended food. Admire them; please don’t feed them.

Petanu River Walk

~1 hour there and back · Go any time

Down through the gardens to the water, with the story of the river the Balinese still call cursed.

Pejeng Kawan

All around us · Walk out any time

A working village, not a set. Offerings on the doorsteps, temple mornings, everyday life at the gate. The carving workshops are further out: a short drive, not a stroll.

Five experiences, every one of them ours, from a sunrise at Mount Batur to a night across the water on Nusa Penida.

Hidden Gem Spa
& the riverside shala

The treatment rooms are open on one side to the gorge, so the massage comes with the sound of the water eighty metres below. Rituals are built on the royal Balinese formulas: turmeric, ginger, frangipani and sandalwood, ground on the property.
